# 01 — Market Research and Validation

## Evidence versus hypothesis
Ipsos Indonesia's 2023 short-video research summary describes short video as a significant media-consumption format; it is a directional, dated signal, not a current market-size estimate: https://www.ipsos.com/en-id/uncovering-growth-short-video-indonesia
A 2026 secondary industry summary describes a fragmented social platform landscape and video-first behavior; verify underlying datasets before using its figures commercially: https://beriklan.co.id/riset/laporan-industri-iklan-digital-2026/
Euromonitor's 2025 Indonesia filmmaking overview is broad industry context, not proof that small businesses will buy Embrilo packs: https://www.euromonitor.com/filmmaking-in-indonesia-isic-9211/report

## Customer hypotheses
1. Indonesian micro/small businesses needing consistent content but lacking a content strategist.
2. Solo creators and affiliate marketers needing product scripts.
3. Service businesses needing localized Bahasa Indonesia storytelling.

## Pain hypotheses to validate
- Planning posts takes too long.
- Hooks and scripts are hard to write consistently.
- AI drafts feel generic and require editing.
- Agency services may be too broad or expensive for early-stage businesses.

## Alternatives
DIY AI tools, freelancers, agencies, Canva/template packs, and in-house creation. Do not claim Embrilo is better without comparing real offers and results.

## 14-day validation
1. Collect 15–20 relevant public examples from permitted sources.
2. Interview 8–12 buyers about their last real content task, current workaround, time/cost, and past spending.
3. Manually fulfil 3–5 pilot packs.
4. Measure willingness to pay, time to produce, acceptance, revisions, and repeat requests.
5. Compare script packs, product storytelling packs, and weekly content plans.
6. Treat demand as validated only after real use/payment or a clearly documented commitment—not compliments.

Positioning candidate: “Turn a product brief into practical short-form content, with AI assistance and human review.” Do not promise virality or sales outcomes.
